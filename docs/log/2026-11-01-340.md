# 2026-11-01: Fix

structure 0.2.9, issue 340. `check-file-size` skips a line of `.file-size-excludes` over 512 bytes, so a long line no longer stalls it for 25 s or fails it with exit 126. A glob of 513 to 4096 bytes used to count and now does not; what it excluded shows up in the next report. `check-fan-out` now counts a line's bytes before it trims, as `check-file-size` does, so a short glob padded past 512 bytes is skipped in both. The copies in other repos differ until their next sync. Pages changed: `reference/plugins/structure.md`.
