# 2026-11-01: Fix

structure 0.2.7, issue 337. `structure-hook`, `check-fan-out` and `check-file-size` now work in a repo whose top ends in a newline, and the hook also from a folder whose name ends in one. A folder with a newline in its name is now checked, and counted apart from any other. `check-fan-out` writes such a name with `\n` and a backslash as `\\`, in reports and in `.fan-out-baseline`. The copies in other repos differ until their next sync. Pages changed: `reference/plugins/structure.md`.
