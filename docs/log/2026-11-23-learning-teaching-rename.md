# 2026-11-23: Update

learning 0.6.0: `quizzing-a-topic` and `quizzing-the-session` are renamed `teaching-a-topic` and `teaching-the-session`, because both teach before they test. The old names are gone and were not archived in `deprecated`, since nothing but the name changed. `teaching-a-topic` now also challenges some correct answers so the learner has to defend them, re-tests items demonstrated earlier in the session without warning and reopens an item the learner fails, and poses one integrative problem across at least two pillars before the finish gate. The three ideas come from Jane Street's `socratic-tutor` skill; its level scale and progress map were left out. Changed `reference/plugins/learning.md`, `reference/skills.md`, `explanation/ports-and-licenses.md` and `explanation/repo-conventions.md`.
