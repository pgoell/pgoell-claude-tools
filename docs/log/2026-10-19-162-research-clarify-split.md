# 2026-10-19: Update

research 2.3.0, workbench 1.4.7. Issue #162 checked three skills that ask questions and then build, `research:research`, `writing:ghostwrite` and `presentations:designing-presentations`, for a clarify phase that rushes to the work. The check read two transcript samples per skill; it ran no paid evals. `research:research` rushed in 2 of 2 samples, so it is now two skills: `research:research` asks one question at a time, writes `brief.md` and waits for the user's approval, then recommends `research:running-research`, which plans, searches and writes the report from the approved brief and stops without one. The prompt files, recipes and report templates moved to `skills/running-research/`. `writing:ghostwrite` and `presentations:designing-presentations` did not rush and stay unchanged: their interview asks one question at a time and gates on an outline or brief before any work. Workbench's design-system files and config schema now name `research:running-research` as the HTML producer.
