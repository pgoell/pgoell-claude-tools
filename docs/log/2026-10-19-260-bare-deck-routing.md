# 2026-10-19: Fix

presentations 0.7.6, issue #260. A bare ask for a deck, such as "I need a deck about our Q3 results." with no `deck.md`, no settled storyline and no content, went to `creating-presentations` and skipped the intake of `designing-presentations`. The descriptions of `designing-presentations` and `creating-presentations` now send such an ask to `designing-presentations` first; a ready `deck.md`, a settled storyline, or an ask that carries its own content still goes to `creating-presentations`. The routing line in the `creating-presentations` SKILL.md now tells those three states apart. New eval cases 13 (`13-bare-ask-routes-to-designing`) and 14 (`14-settled-storyline-routes-to-creating`) in `plugins/presentations/evals/`, and case 11 gains a grader that checks `creating-presentations` did not fire. Case 12 (figures attached) now asks to plan the storyline first, so it still routes to `designing-presentations`. `docs/reference/plugins/presentations.md` changed.
