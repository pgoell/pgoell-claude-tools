# 2026-11-01: Fix

structure 0.2.10, issues 342 and 344. Both checks now skip a line of their excludes file with more than 4 `**`, and every glob after the 1024th; comments, blank lines and skipped lines do not count. So 1 MiB of short lines no longer fails `check-file-size` with exit 126, and one line of `**/` no longer stalls git or mawk. In `.fan-out-excludes` a run of `*` now reads as `**`, as in git, so `***/x` also matches `x` at the root. A longer list or a starrier glob used to count and now does not; what it excluded shows up in the next report. The lint job of `pr-validation.yml` and the workflow in `references/ci.md` set `timeout-minutes`. The copies in other repos differ until their next sync. Pages changed: `reference/plugins/structure.md`.
