# 2026-11-01: Fix

`structure` 0.2.11, issue 345. `check-fan-out` no longer builds a regex from a glob: mawk backtracked for minutes on many `*` in one glob and on `**/**/**/**/zz` over a very deep path. A matcher of its own in the awk keeps the glob meaning under gawk, mawk and busybox awk. Under gawk in a UTF-8 locale, a glob now matches a name that is not valid UTF-8, as it did under the other two. Changed pages: `reference/plugins/structure.md`.
