# 2026-11-01: Fix

structure 0.2.4, issues 326 and 327. `structure-hook` could hang on a repo with a FIFO or a symlink to a device at a file it reads, `gawk` could fill memory, and a SIGTERM left children running. With `.` or an empty entry on `PATH` it ran that repo's tools, and `env bash` could find a `bash` there. The checks now read their settings only from regular files, up to 1 MiB. The hook starts as `#!/bin/sh`, drops every `PATH` entry that does not start with `/`, runs git under a 4 GiB memory limit, and kills all it started after 30 s or on SIGTERM. On that kill it exits 1 with `structure-hook: gave up on <file>`. The copy in `pgoell/project-template` differs until its next sync. Pages changed: `reference/plugins/structure.md`.
