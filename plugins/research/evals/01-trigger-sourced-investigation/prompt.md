Investigate whether the EU AI Act's obligations for general-purpose AI models apply to a German mid-sized company that fine-tunes an open-weight model for internal HR screening. Gather many sources (the regulation text, Commission guidance, legal analyses), synthesize what the obligations are and where experts disagree, have the conclusions checked by a reviewer before you finalize, and write a final report with citations. The audience is our legal and engineering leads, the scope is the obligations in force by the end of 2027, and the report should run to about 2000 words.
