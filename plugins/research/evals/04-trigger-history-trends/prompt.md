Trace the history and trends of web accessibility practice from WCAG 1.0 in 1999 to today: how the standards changed (WCAG 2.0, 2.1, 2.2 and the WCAG 3 drafts), how laws such as Section 508 and the European Accessibility Act drove adoption, and what large audits of popular websites show about progress over time. Synthesize what you find across many sources into a final report with citations, and say where the evidence is thin. The audience is our product and design leads, and the report should run to about 2000 words.
