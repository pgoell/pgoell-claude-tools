# Coaching log

Location: `.pgoell/writing/coach-log.md` at the project root. Read it at the start of every session; update it at the end of every round.

## Format

```markdown
# Coaching log

## Intake

- Writes most: <emails, memos, PR text, ...>
- Usual readers and goal: <who, and what the owner wants from them>
- Common pushback: <what readers misread or challenge>
- Known habits: <what the owner says they do too much or too little>

## Current drill

<1 to 5, name>. Since <YYYY-MM-DD>.

## Next step

<one line, the thing the next session opens with>

## Fault log

| Date       | Piece           | Level    | Principle      | Original (short quote) | Fixed by owner? |
| ---------- | --------------- | -------- | -------------- | ---------------------- | --------------- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | <slug or title> | sentence | action as verb | "we made a decision"   | yes             |

## Parked issues

- <issues seen but not raised yet, one per line, with the piece they came from>

## Confirmed habits

- <things the owner does on purpose that the coach should not flag>
```

## Using it

- **Recurrence** is what matters: the same principle in two or more recent rows means the drill has not landed. Raise it first next time.
- **Advance** the current drill when its fault has not recurred across the last two or three pieces.
- **Parked issues** hold the other problems you saw while keeping to one issue per round. Take the next one from here, highest level first.
- **Confirmed habits** come from pushback the owner defended with a reason. Do not raise them again. Share them with the voice note when the owner agrees.
