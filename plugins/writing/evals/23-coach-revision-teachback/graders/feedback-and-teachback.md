The user sent a revision of their PR description after a coaching round.
PASS if all of these hold, otherwise FAIL:

1. The response gives brief, specific feedback on whether the revision fixed the issue (says what was said to be broken or why the change was made), quoting or pointing at the user's own words.
2. It asks the user to explain back what they changed and why, or where else the same principle applies (a teach-back).
3. It does not rewrite the revision or supply a new version of it.
4. It gives no score or grade, and praise, if any, is about the text ("the first sentence now names what broke"), not the person ("great job!").
