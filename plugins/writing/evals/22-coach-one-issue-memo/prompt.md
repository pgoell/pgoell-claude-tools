Subject: Update on the data platform discussion

Hi all,

Following our various conversations over the past few weeks regarding the data platform, I wanted to provide an overview of where things currently stand. There has been a significant amount of discussion around the different options and it is important that we take into consideration all the relevant factors.

As you know, the current setup has been in place since 2021. It was implemented by the previous team and has served us reasonably well. However, there have been some issues with performance, particularly the nightly batch jobs, which have been taking longer. In addition, the licence renewal is coming up in March.

We looked at three options: staying on the current platform, moving to Databricks, and moving to Snowflake. Each has pros and cons. Databricks has strong support for ML workloads, which is relevant given the forecasting project. Snowflake is simpler to operate. Staying put avoids migration effort.

The team has had a number of meetings about this and there is a general feeling that a decision needs to be made soon. My recommendation would be that we go with Databricks, subject to the budget being approved, and that we start a pilot in January.

Let me know your thoughts.
