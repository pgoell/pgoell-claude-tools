This is one slide of a corporate presentation. The target register is "editorial corporate": professional enough for a client boardroom, modern enough not to look like a 2012 template. Score each claim true or false from what is visible, then give the fraction true as the score.

a. The slide has one clear focal element (a headline, a hero number, or one diagram) that the eye lands on first.
b. No text is clipped, cut off at an edge, overlapping another element, or running outside the slide.
c. There are no gradients, glassmorphism or frosted panels, neon or glowing colors, emoji, or playful heavily rounded cards.
d. It is not a grey-box flowchart, SmartArt-style diagram, or a stack of more than five bullets.
e. The accent color is applied to at most one group of elements; nothing is highlighted without an evident reason.
f. Elements that are siblings (columns, cards, list items) share the same type size and weight.
g. Whitespace looks deliberate: no large dead area (for example an empty bottom half) and no crammed edges.
