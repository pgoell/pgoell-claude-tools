Growth on the protected corridors did not come from nearby painted routes

Painted corridors held at about 3k trips a day while protected ones rose to 12k, so riders did not just move over.

How we counted

14 counters on 10 corridors, weekday averages each January

When lanes changed

Protection came in stages, from April 2022 to September 2023

What we cannot say

Counters record trips, not people, so new riders and extra trips look the same
