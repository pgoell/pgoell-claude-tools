Protected lanes tripled winter riding and halved collisions on the six corridors

Trips

  • Winter trips on the 6 protected corridors rose from 4k to 12k a day
  • Protected corridors now lose a quarter of riders in winter, not half

Streets

  • Collisions involving cyclists fell by half on the protected corridors
  • Car trips on the corridors fell from 96k to 90k a day

Riders

  • Half of all winter trips fall in four commuting hours
  • Brenford keeps 66% of summer riders in winter, third of 12 cities
Sources: Brenford bike counters, 2018 to 2025; police collision records; city traffic counts 18
