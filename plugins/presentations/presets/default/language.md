# Language: default preset

Applies to every written surface of a deck: titles, body, labels, source lines, and speaker notes. Presets without their own `language.md` use this one.

## Style contract

- **Everyday words.** Use the word the audience would say in the meeting.
- **Active voice, present tense.** Name who does what: "One catalog sets access rules".
- **Sentence case** for titles, labels, and column heads.
- **Numerals** for counts and measures: 3 workloads, 12 weeks, $40M.
- **One claim per title, with its proof inside it.** The proof is a number, an actor, or a comparison taken from the source. Keep titles to about 15 words and two lines.
- **End on the last concrete fact.** Stop the sentence when the proof is stated.
- **Contrast only to correct a belief the audience holds.** Name the belief in the slide or the notes; otherwise state the claim on its own.
- **Short lists.** 2 to 4 items, each at most two lines, left aligned, plain weight.
- **A source line under every number**, in the form `Source: <who>, <what>, <when>`.

## Facts

Use only facts, figures, names, and arithmetic that appear in the source material. If a title needs a number the source lacks, write the title with what the source does give and tell the user what is missing. Sums, percentages, and growth rates count as new figures unless the source states them.
