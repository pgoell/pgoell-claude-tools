Instead write a short decision doc directly to `/tmp/<project-name>-pilot/decision.md`: topic, options considered, decisions, risks. No simulated Q&A, no format-verify grep. If the topic is too big for one spec, decompose it, record the chosen sub-topic and the reasoning in the deviation log, and continue with that sub-topic.
