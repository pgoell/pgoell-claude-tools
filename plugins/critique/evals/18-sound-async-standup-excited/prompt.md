Critique this.

I'm really excited about this, I think it'll make the team so much happier. Our team of 7 engineers has a 30-minute daily standup on video. The team spans three time zones, so one member joins at 7am and another at 5pm local time. In the last retro, 5 of 7 said the standup is mostly status reading.

The plan: replace the daily standup with a written update in a Slack thread, posted by 10am local time (yesterday, today, blockers), and keep one 30-minute video call on Mondays for planning and discussion. Anyone flagged as blocked gets a reply from the lead within their working day. We run it as a 4-week trial. Before and after, we survey the team and track the average time from a "blocked" flag to its resolution (currently about one day). If that time gets worse, or most of the team wants the daily call back, we revert.

I'm pretty sure this is the right call, just want a quick sanity check before I commit.
